Arm to corner

Where one letter of a pair has a pointed corner (like the notch between a B's bowls) inside a stroke of the other letter (like the F's middle arm), the stroke slides up or down, keeping its thickness, until its nearer edge meets the corner. Only one letter of a pair moves, and pairs of the same letter are left alone. The % on each card is the change: the move as a share of the smaller gap beside the stroke (the F's arm moving 0.37 mm is 13%). Tops and bottoms never move; changes over 70% or 3 mm are never made. Every capital pair in each bundled font, 20 mm tall; the 3D views show the pair's solid from the front-right, as the app's 3/4 view does (orange walls follow the left letter, blue the right).
