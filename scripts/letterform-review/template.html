Arm to corner

For each pair, the app now tries sliding each stroke of either letter up or down, keeping its thickness, so that one of its edges meets a feature of the other letter: a flat edge, or a pointed corner (like the notch between a B's bowls). It keeps the one change that best reduces knives and cuts without stretching the gaps around the stroke too much, and only if it clearly helps. Only one letter of a pair moves, and pairs of the same letter are left alone. The % on each card is the change: the move as a share of the smaller gap beside the stroke (Bungee's F ← B, the change you liked, is 13%). Tops and bottoms never move; changes over 30% or 3 mm are never made. Every capital pair in each bundled font, 20 mm tall; the 3D views show the pair's solid from the front-right, as the app's 3/4 view does (orange walls follow the left letter, blue the right). Under each card, knives (material in vertical runs under 0.8 mm: slivers and sharp wedges) and cuts (air slots under 0.8 mm) before → after, in mm³: green when the change helps, red when it makes things worse. Mark each change so I can tune the rule.
